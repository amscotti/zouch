# ADR 0002 — Tier 1 is a replication peer, not a drop-in clone

**Status:** accepted · 2026-10-31

## Context

CouchDB is roughly 200k lines of Erlang. A drop-in replacement with clustering is a multi-year
project; a replication peer is a few person-months.

## Decision

The v1 contract is **Tier 1**: CouchDB and PouchDB can push to and pull from Zouch, with correct
documents, revisions, conflicts, attachments (later) and checkpoints. Views and Mango are Tier 2 and
phased in afterwards. Clustering (Tier 3) is explicitly out of scope forever.

## Consequences

Compatibility is measured by executable tests against a real CouchDB, not by endpoint count.
CouchDB and PouchDB initiate replication by default. Zouch also exposes `POST /_replicate` for
one-shot local pulls and pushes, and the `_replicator` scheduler runs local-target pulls or
local-to-remote pushes.
